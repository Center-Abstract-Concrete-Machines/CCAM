JACKIE TURPIN is a multimedia artist, filmmaker, and designer based in Los angeles, CA. Through an alchemy of experimental digital approaches and a deep knowledge of traditional animation technique Jackie liquifies identity, media history and pop culture to conjure work that spans film, installation, and performance. Evoking dreams or half forgotten memories she weaves these expressions with an earnest humor that result in kinetic tapestries of emotion and color. Jackie’s work has been showcased in such venues as LA County Museum of Art, Ottawa International Animation Festival, Slamdance, K Museum Seoul and others. Jackie earned her BFA from CalArts and MFA from UCLA.
