James Connolly is a Chicago-based artist, educator, archivist, and developer. His videos, open-source tools, and real-time audio/video performances undermine the interfaces and break through the algorithms of digital and analog systems, examining hidden power structures and liberating latent aesthetic materialities in cathartic and captivating compositions. He has exhibited at the Museum of Contemporary Art Chicago; Currents New Media Festival in Santa Fe; Ann Arbor Film Festival; Intermediale's Videosyntezy3 in Legnica, Poland; London Experimental Film Festival; Chicago Underground Film Festival; Bideodromo International Experimental Film and Video Festival in Bilbao, Spain; South by Southwest in Austin; the GLI.TC/H Festivals in Chicago; and the Vancouver New Music Festival, among others. He is an Associate Professor, Adj. in the departments of Film, Video, New Media, and Animation and Art History, Theory, and Criticism at the School of the Art Institute of Chicago.
