Ralph Darden is a Chicago-based musician, DJ, composer & Educator

For nearly 3 decades, Ralph has been writing music, recording & performing in various touring bands. He currently plays guitar and sings backing vocals for Ted Leo & The Pharmacists.

In 2021, he began his foray into the world of collaborative improvisation by live scoring a piece by artist Yasmin Spiro at the Hyde Park Arts Center. He has since participated in various improvisational performances at Chicago’s Elastic Arts Center.

Darden composes music for films & television. Some examples of his previous work include Polish Bar (2010), The Inheritance (2011), Lac Du Flambeau (2012), Comcast’s Gross World (2012) and Mondo Media’s Happy Tree Friends (2015)

He has Dj’d events around the globe under the moniker of DJ Major Taylor. His scratch-laden, dance-friendly, genre-defying sets showcase an ability to weave his eclectic palate of influences into a sonic tapestry, distinctly his own.

Ralph is also a music instructor at Bandwith Chicago a non profit origination that provides quality music education to children in underserved communities
