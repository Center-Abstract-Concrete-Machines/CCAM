## ARTIST BIO

Fleming is a queer multidisciplinary artist working at the intersection of somatics, sound, politics, and new media. Throughout their multi-faceted practice they are exploring embodiment as a tool for collective liberation. They are one half of the tekno duo 404 NOT FOUND and a long term member of a network of DIY underground creative communities in the United States, in which care and organizing are an important part of their practice. They currently work and reside in Chicago, Illinois, where they are faculty at the School of the Art Institute of Chicago.
